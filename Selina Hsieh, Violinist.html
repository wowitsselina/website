About Selina

Selina has played the violin since the age of five. She is receiving her Bachelors of Music in Violin Performance from the University of California, Irvine. She is regularly seen performing at Winifred and Barclay Theater and serves as the concertmaster of UCI Symphony Orchestra.

Selina lives and teaches in Irvine, California. Whether she is on stage or in the studio, her aim is the same: to make the violin feel warm, expressive and within reach.
